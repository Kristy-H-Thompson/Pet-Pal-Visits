Our Services

Heading out on a getaway? Make sure your four-legged buddy gets the VIP treatment at home! Discover the best care options here, so you can travel worry-free while your pet gets plenty of love.

INITIAL CONSULTATION

This complimentary visit is essential for all new clients. We’ll come to your home to spend time with you and your pet(s), allowing you to meet your pet sitter, learn about Pet Pal Visits With Matthew, ask questions, and discuss your pet’s individual needs.

SNUGGLE STOP

Our basic visit includes fresh food, water change, a walk and/or playtime, litter box cleaning, and lots of affection and love! *20-30 minutes in length*

PAWS AND PLAY

Our Paws and Play visit includes everything in our Snuggle Stop, but is extended by thirty minutes so that your pet gets a little extra play time. *60 minutes in length*

OVERNIGHT STAYS

For overnight stays, we come into your home to care for your pet(s), and carry out their normal routine that you perform with them daily. They will have companionship throughout the night, and you will have peace of mind!

WAGS AND WANDER

For dog walks, we come to your home and walk your dog(s) on their normal routine walk. We can also pick your dog(s) up and take them to a doggy park of your choice.
