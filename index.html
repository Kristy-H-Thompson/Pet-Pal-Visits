Our Services

Heading out on a getaway? Make sure your four-legged buddy gets the VIP treatment at home! Discover the best care options here, so you can travel worry-free while your pet gets plenty of love.

INITIAL CONSULTATION

This complimentary visit is essential for all new clients. We’ll come to your home to meet your pet(s), discuss their needs, and answer any questions you have.

SNUGGLE STOP

Our basic visit includes fresh food, water, a walk and/or playtime, litter box cleaning, and lots of affection! *20-30 minutes in length*

PAWS AND PLAY

Includes everything in our Snuggle Stop but extended by 30 minutes for extra playtime. *60 minutes in length*

OVERNIGHT STAYS

We stay overnight to care for your pet(s), following their usual routine to ensure they feel safe and loved.

WAGS AND WANDER

We come to your home and take your dog(s) for a walk or a trip to their favorite dog park.
